# Best WhatsApp CRM In 2026 (India): Top Tools Compared

You already run a CRM. Zoho, maybe Salesforce, Bitrix24 or HubSpot. Your reps already close on WhatsApp. And you already spend on Meta and Instagram ads to fill the pipe.

So when you go looking for the **best WhatsApp CRM**, you're not shopping for a new system. You're trying to connect the WhatsApp your team already lives in to the CRM you already pay for — without two things that keep coming up in every buying conversation we have.

> "Can WhatsApp numbers auto-sync and match with our Salesforce contact and lead properties, or is that manual?" — *Sales lead, 30-seat B2B team*

> "Can personal WhatsApp and the WhatsApp Business API coexist, so we keep informal chats but add automation?" — *Same team, evaluating a switch*

Two fears sit underneath almost every question: **migrating the number** reps have used for years, and **getting the number banned** for bulk sending. A real estate sales head put the visibility problem more bluntly: *"These are all going in different directions — one is not integrated with the other,"* and managers *"can't see who replied or dropped a lead."*

This guide ranks the best WhatsApp CRM tools for 2026 through an India-first lens — real per-seat costs, migration risk, and how deep the CRM sync actually goes. Eazybe comes first (it's what we build), but every competitor entry below is factual, sourced, and fair.

## What Makes A Good WhatsApp CRM In 2026?

A **WhatsApp CRM** connects your WhatsApp conversations to your customer records so that every chat, contact, and follow-up lives where your sales team already works. The best WhatsApp CRM in 2026 doesn't replace your CRM — it makes WhatsApp a first-class channel inside it.

Here's the buying criteria we'd lead with, in order:

1. **Coexistence and no migration.** Can reps keep their existing number and the free WhatsApp Business App / Web, or does the tool force everyone onto the Business API? Coexistence lets you add automation *without* changing numbers — increasingly important because [Meta starts charging for service messages from October 1, 2026](/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-changes-2026), so per-conversation models get pricier. (See our [WhatsApp coexistence guide](/blog/whatsapp-coexistence) for how the Business App + Cloud API combination works.)

2. **Two-way CRM sync, not shallow logging.** Do WhatsApp contacts auto-match to CRM records by phone number? Do chats, attachments, and AI-written properties flow *back* into the CRM automatically — or does someone copy-paste?

3. **Per-seat pricing with no per-conversation markup.** API-BSP platforms bill you for every conversation *plus* a markup on Meta's rates. A flat per-seat model is predictable as volume grows.

4. **Ban-safety and channel flexibility.** Does the tool support personal WhatsApp, the Business App, *and* the API — without pushing you into risky high-volume blasting that gets numbers flagged?

5. **Visibility for managers.** Can a manager see who replied, who dropped a lead, and how fast the team responds? A shared inbox with assignment and analytics beats a black box.

6. **Depth of AI.** Genuinely generative replies for off-script conversations, or rigid templated "bot" flows?

## WhatsApp CRM Pricing In India: What To Expect

Pricing in India splits cleanly into two models, and getting this right saves the most money:

**Per-seat (flat).** You pay per user, per month, regardless of message volume. Eazybe, TimelinesAI, and Kommo work this way. Predictable — your bill doesn't spike with a busy campaign month.

**Per-conversation (usage-based).** API-BSP platforms — WATI, AiSensy, Interakt, DoubleTick — charge a subscription **plus** Meta's per-conversation fees, often with a markup on top. The advertised ₹/$ base price is rarely the real bill. And with [Meta charging for service messages from October 1, 2026](/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-changes-2026), the per-conversation model gets more expensive for support-heavy teams.

Rules of thumb for Indian buyers:

- **Never take the advertised base as the all-in cost** for any API platform. Add Meta's per-conversation fees, plus per-user, chatbot-session and integration add-ons.
- **Watch for minimum commitments.** Kommo has a 6-month minimum; DoubleTick is annual-only. Eazybe and TimelinesAI offer trials without a long lock-in.
- **If your volume is high and growing,** flat per-seat almost always wins on total cost.
- **If you're a pure marketing/broadcast shop,** a usage-based API platform may still fit — just model the conversation fees honestly.

**What is the best WhatsApp CRM in 2026?**

**Do I have to migrate my WhatsApp number to use a WhatsApp CRM?**
No. With Eazybe's **Coexistence mode**, reps keep their existing number and the free WhatsApp Business App / Web, and you can add Cloud API automation without changing numbers. API-only platforms like WATI, AiSensy, Interakt and DoubleTick require WhatsApp Business API onboarding first.

API-BSP platforms advertise a base subscription but bill Meta's per-conversation fees — often with a markup — on top, so the real bill scales with volume. **Will a WhatsApp CRM get my number banned?**
Bans are tied to Meta's bulk-messaging policy, not to using a CRM. The risk rises with high-volume broadcasting. Eazybe is built around CRM sync and rep productivity over WhatsApp Web rather than mass blasting, which reduces that exposure — but no tool can override Meta's rules, so follow opt-in and volume best practices.

**What's the difference between a WhatsApp CRM and a broadcast tool?**
A WhatsApp CRM (Eazybe, TimelinesAI, Kommo) keeps individual sales conversations synced to customer records for reps and managers. A broadcast tool (AiSensy, Interakt, and the broadcast side of WATI/DoubleTick) is optimized for one-to-many marketing campaigns billed per conversation. Many teams need one, not both — pick by whether your priority is sales conversations or marketing reach.
