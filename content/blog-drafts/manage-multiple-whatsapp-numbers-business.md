## What Does "Managing Multiple WhatsApp Numbers" Mean?

Managing multiple WhatsApp numbers for business means connecting two or more WhatsApp accounts—whether they run on the **WhatsApp Business App**, **WhatsApp Business API (WABA)**, or even **personal WhatsApp**—to a single shared inbox. Each number keeps its own identity (name, profile picture, verification badge), but your team views and replies to every chat from one interface.

This is not the same as switching accounts on your phone. A true multi-number inbox:
- Shows all conversations side-by-side, labeled by number.
- Lets you assign chats to specific agents.
- Applies filters (by channel, messaging window, AI properties) across every connected number.
- Syncs contact data and chat history to your CRM, regardless of which number received the message.

## Why Businesses Need to Manage Multiple WhatsApp Numbers

### 1. Regional Teams & Franchises
A retail chain with stores in three states might give each location its own WhatsApp number for local promotions. Headquarters needs a bird's-eye view—without logging into three separate phones.

### 2. Departmental Routing
Sales, support, and billing each get a dedicated number. Customers see a purpose-specific contact, and the back-office team routes chats to the right queue without sharing a single phone.

### 3. WhatsApp Business API Coexistence
Meta's [coexistence model](https://business.whatsapp.com/products/business-platform) lets you keep your number on the WhatsApp Business App (for 1:1 quick replies) while also connecting it to the API (for automation and CRM integrations). Managing both channels in one inbox prevents duplicate replies and siloed workflows.

### 4. International Expansion
When you open an office in Mexico, you provision a local +52 number to build trust. Your existing +1 U.S. number continues to serve North American customers. A multi-number inbox unifies both queues under one team calendar and one set of analytics.

## Honest Limits: What a Multi-Number Inbox Can't Do

### 1. AI Is Assistive, Not Autonomous
Eazybe's AI Sales Briefs (Summary, Intent, Urgency) require a human to review and act. The system does not auto-reply or auto-assign without your configuration. You set the rules (e.g., "Assign high-urgency chats to Manager A"), and the AI provides the properties.

### 2. Dynamic Labels Are One-Directional (HubSpot/Zoho Only)
If your CRM updates a deal stage, the inbox reflects it as a label within ~3 minutes. But creating a label manually in Eazybe does not push a new property back to HubSpot or Zoho. (Two-way contact sync—name, email, phone—works in both directions; property sync is CRM-to-Eazybe only.)

### 3. WhatsApp Business API Incurs Per-Message Charges
Meta's [pricing (effective November 1, 2025)](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing) applies to WABA conversations. The WhatsApp Business App and personal WhatsApp channels remain free. Eazybe charges per number, not per user, but you still pay Meta's per-conversation fee for WABA numbers.

### 4. Initial CRM Backfill Is Limited
When you first connect HubSpot or Zoho, Eazybe syncs the last 3 days of contacts to avoid quota overruns. Historical contacts require a manual CSV import or incremental sync.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Can I connect both WhatsApp Business App and WhatsApp Business API on the same number?
Yes—this is called **coexistence**. Meta allows one number to run on the Business App (for quick 1:1 replies) and the API (for automation and CRM integrations) simultaneously. Eazybe displays both channels in the same inbox, tagged separately, so you see which platform sent each message.

### 2. How many WhatsApp numbers can I connect?
Unlimited. Eazybe charges per number, so you can scale from two to twenty without changing the core inbox experience. (Competitors often cap connections at 5 or 10 per plan tier.)

### 3. Do all agents see all numbers, or can I restrict visibility?
You can assign agents to specific numbers under **Team Settings** > **Members**. For example, support agents see only the support-queue number, while managers see all channels.

### 4. What happens if two agents reply to the same chat from different numbers?
The inbox shows real-time typing indicators and locks a chat when one agent is composing a reply. If you have the same contact chatting on two different numbers, those appear as separate conversations (because WhatsApp treats each number as a distinct endpoint).

### 5. Does Eazybe store my chat data?
No. Chats live in your CRM (HubSpot, Zoho) or Google Sheet/Drive. Eazybe reads and displays the data through Meta's WhatsApp Web interface or the Cloud API, but does not persist messages on Eazybe servers. (SOC 2 Type II, GDPR compliant.)

### 6. Can I filter the inbox by a HubSpot deal stage or custom property?
Yes—via **Dynamic Labels**. When a HubSpot property (e.g., `dealstage`) changes, Eazybe auto-applies a label to the chat. You then filter the inbox by that label. (This is a CRM-to-Eazybe one-directional sync, not a native search of HubSpot fields inside the inbox.)

### 7. How fast is the CRM sync?
- **Contacts**: ~3 minutes for HubSpot; ~15 minutes for Zoho.
- **Properties (Dynamic Labels)**: Real-time to ~3 minutes.
- **Initial backfill**: Last 3 days of contacts.

### 8. What if I already use WhatsApp Web's multi-account feature (since 2023)?
WhatsApp Web lets you open multiple accounts in separate browser profiles or windows, but you still switch tabs manually. A Team Inbox like Eazybe unifies all chats in one view, adds assignment, filters, and CRM sync—so you don't need five browser windows.

Ensure the platform supports all three: personal WhatsApp (QR), WhatsApp Business App (QR), and WABA (Cloud API or on-premise). Some tools lock you into API-only, which forces you to migrate every number to WABA.

### CRM Integration Depth
Look for two-way sync (contacts) and at least one-directional property sync (CRM deal stages → inbox labels). Ask whether chat data lives on the vendor's servers or in your own CRM/Drive.

### AI Capabilities
Check whether AI is **assistive** (suggests intent, urgency, next action for you to review) or **autonomous** (auto-assigns or auto-replies). Most compliance-conscious teams prefer assistive AI with a human-in-the-loop.

### Data Residency & Compliance
If you operate in the EU, healthcare, or finance, confirm SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, or HIPAA compliance. Ask where chat transcripts are stored (on vendor servers, in Meta's cloud, or only in your CRM).
