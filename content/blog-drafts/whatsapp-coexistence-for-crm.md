## What Is WhatsApp Coexistence (In Plain English)?

**WhatsApp Coexistence** is a Meta-provided feature that connects your active **WhatsApp Business App** phone number to the **WhatsApp Cloud API** — without losing your chats or contacts, and without re-registering the number. Meta positions it as _the safest method to reach the Cloud API without losing data_.

The word that matters is *coexistence*: the same number lives in **two places at once**. Your team keeps using the WhatsApp Business App on the phone exactly as before, while the API layer runs in parallel. Messages and contacts sync in real time in both directions — a message you send from the app appears in your API-connected inbox, and a message sent through the API shows up in the app.

Here's the mental model buyers keep asking for, captured by one prospect who was juggling five disconnected tools: _"these are all going in different directions, one is not integrated with the other."_ Coexistence is what makes them one thing. You get:

- **One number.** No new number, no "message us on our new WhatsApp" awkwardness with customers who already know you.
- **Manual WhatsApp intact.** Reps still tap out human replies in the app. Nothing is deleted; existing chats remain.
- **An API layer on top.** That's what unlocks CRM sync, broadcasts with delivery tracking, and optional AI agents.

Nothing about the customer's experience changes. They still message the same number and get replies in the same thread. What changes is everything happening behind the glass on your side.

For years, incoming customer replies and "service" conversations on the WhatsApp API were effectively free within the 24-hour window. That's changing. For high-volume support and sales teams, that's a real line item. A team fielding hundreds of inbound service conversations a day on the API is now paying per message for work that used to be free.

Coexistence gives you a lever. You reserve the API for what genuinely needs it — approved broadcast templates, automation, AI agents — and keep the free channel for everything else.

That's the founder-flagged reason Coexistence suddenly got popular. It's not just "keep my number." It's **keep my number *and* keep the free channel** so a Meta pricing change doesn't quietly inflate your monthly bill.

To be precise: Coexistence itself carries **no additional Eazybe or Meta fee**. Standard Meta messaging fees still apply to whatever you actually send through the API, and your Eazybe subscription applies as normal. The savings come from *not* pushing every human reply through paid API messaging.

## Coexistence vs Full API Migration

The old path to the WhatsApp API was a **full migration**: you took your number off the WhatsApp Business App, registered it on a Business Solution Provider's API, and your team lost the familiar app interface for day-to-day chat. That's exactly the move buyers dread — and the reason so many stall before ever automating anything.

Coexistence is the alternative. Here's the honest side-by-side.

| | **WhatsApp Coexistence** | **Full API Migration** |

| **Keep your number** | Yes — same number, no re-registration | Yes, but the number leaves the Business App |
| **Keep the WhatsApp Business App** | Yes — app keeps working exactly as before | No — the number moves fully to the API |

| **Reconnect cooldown** | A **1–2 month** cooldown applies if you unlink and want to reconnect | N/A in the same way |

Coexistence isn't "better" in every dimension — a pure API setup can make sense for a business that broadcasts at massive scale and wants no app at all. But for the overwhelming majority of teams we talk to, the ask is _"one solution for everything… everything in a single window"_ without ripping out how reps already work. That's the Coexistence sweet spot.

## Eligibility: Can You Enable Coexistence?

Coexistence has real prerequisites. Meta gates it to protect number health, so it's worth checking each box before you start. Here's the eligibility list, straight from the requirements:

- **A WhatsApp Business App number.** The number must be registered on the **WhatsApp Business App**. Personal WhatsApp numbers are **not supported** — this is the single most common disqualifier. (One prospect asked directly, _"can personal WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business API coexist?"_ — the number has to be on the Business App first.)
- **WhatsApp Business App version 2.24.17 or newer.** Older versions fail during the QR-linking step. Update before you begin.
- **A verified Meta Business Account** with completed business verification.
- **A Facebook Business Page** with Admin access.
- **A healthy, active number.** No Meta restrictions, no policy violations, and genuine usage — real one-to-one conversations and consistent activity — for at least **3–7 days before and after** you connect Coexistence.
- **Not already linked to another API provider.** The number must not currently be connected to a different WhatsApp API partner, and must not be blocked by Meta.

Two caveats worth flagging up front, honestly:

- Numbers **from Nigeria or South Africa** may be subject to additional Meta regulations and restrictions. Don't assume every country is equal here.
- Meta's docs recommend **creating a new WABA (WhatsApp Business Account)** for the Coexistence setup, which tends to give the cleanest, healthiest connection flow.

If you tick those boxes, you're eligible. If the number is a personal WhatsApp line, move it to the Business App first, warm it up for a few days, then come back.

## What Imports (And What Doesn't)

This is the section buyers scrutinize hardest, because it's where the "will I lose my data?" fear lives. Here's exactly what Coexistence brings across when you connect:

**Imports automatically:**

- **All your contacts.** Every contact comes over — no manual re-adding.

**Imports optionally (you choose at setup):**

- **Up to 6 months of 1:1 chat history.**
- **Up to 2 weeks of media files.**

**Does *not* import:**

- **Group chats.** Group conversations are not carried over during Coexistence onboarding. (If group history matters to you, note that Eazybe can back up group *and* individual chats separately to your own Google Drive via **Team Inbox** — that's a different mechanism from Coexistence import, and worth setting up alongside.)

One important, honest warning: **choose your import options carefully at first signup.** If you skip history import initially and later want it, you have to fully offboard and re-onboard the number to try again — and the backup may not restore cleanly if the wrong option was chosen the first time. Decide up front whether you want the 6-month chat history and the 2-week media window, and keep the WhatsApp Business App **running during setup** so no messages are lost in the handoff.

## Will This Get My Number Banned?

The honest answer: Coexistence is designed to be safe, and it does **not** get your number banned by itself — but *how you use it* still matters, and no tool can promise immunity from Meta's policies.

Coexistence is, by Meta's own framing, the safe way to add the API without losing data. The bans people fear almost always come from **bulk unsolicited messaging on an unofficial channel**, not from Coexistence. If you want to broadcast to thousands, the official WABA path is the one built to _"send to thousands of users safely"_ — the risky route is blasting from a raw QR-linked number, which Meta docs flag as _"limited & risk of blocking."_

That said, there are specific behaviors that can trigger disconnection on a Coexistence number, and it's fair to know them:

- **Sending bulk unsolicited (spam) messages** that violate WhatsApp Business Policy.
- **Device switching** without a proper migration.
- **Using the mobile app and WhatsApp Web at the same time** in a way that conflicts.
- **Extended inactivity** — no activity for more than **14 days**.
- **Moving the number** back and forth between the consumer WhatsApp app and the Business app.

The safe playbook for the real-estate and outbound teams who ask about this — one wanted to send _"~200 messages/day per number without getting banned"_ — is straightforward: use **official WABA broadcasts with Meta-approved templates**, get **opt-in** before messaging, include an opt-out (_"Reply STOP to opt out"_), warm up new numbers, and keep genuine activity flowing. Do that, and you're on the path Meta considers compliant. Eazybe won't promise "no bans" — nobody credibly can — but pointing volume through official templates instead of unofficial blasting is exactly how you stay clear.
