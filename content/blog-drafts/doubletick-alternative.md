# DoubleTick Alternative in 2026: Why Teams Switch to Eazybe

You bought DoubleTick to broadcast on WhatsApp. It does that well. But six months in, your sales lead is staring at Zoho and asking a different question: _"Why aren't the actual messages getting synced into the CRM?"_

That was almost word-for-word what a Zoho-using team told us on a recent demo call. They ran WhatsApp on DoubleTick, ran their pipeline in Zoho CRM, and the two never really talked. Reps kept flipping between tabs. Managers couldn't see who replied and who dropped a lead. And every broadcast added to a per-conversation bill that nobody had budgeted for.

If that sounds familiar, you're not looking for a better broadcast tool. You're looking for a **DoubleTick alternative** that behaves like part of your CRM. This guide compares the two honestly, shows where DoubleTick genuinely shines, and walks through how CRM-led teams switch to **Eazybe** without migrating their WhatsApp number or rebuilding their workflows.

## Quick Take: Eazybe vs DoubleTick At A Glance

**TL;DR**

- **DoubleTick** is a WhatsApp Business API (WABA) tool built around broadcasts, catalogs, and a shared team inbox — strong for outbound marketing blasts.
- **Eazybe** is a WhatsApp layer built for CRM-led sales teams: **two-way CRM sync**, **no-API Coexistence** (keep your existing number and the free WhatsApp Business App), **Team Inbox with roles**, and broadcasts with **delivery status** when you want them.
- The biggest DoubleTick complaints we hear from switchers: **per-conversation billing**, "it's a broadcast tool, not a CRM," and **thin analytics on lower plans**.
- Eazybe runs your standard Zoho/HubSpot sync over the **Chrome extension on WhatsApp Web** — no WABA required — and adds WABA as an optional layer when you need compliant, large-volume broadcasts.
- You can **bring your WABA number over to Eazybe** and rebuild automations like Zoho payment-link workflows, while keeping manual WhatsApp alive through Coexistence.

The short version: pick DoubleTick if broadcasting is the whole job. Pick Eazybe if your CRM is the center of gravity and WhatsApp needs to feed it.

## What DoubleTick Is Good At (An Honest Look)

A fair comparison starts with what DoubleTick does well, because it does several things well.

- **Broadcast-first design.** DoubleTick is built around sending approved WhatsApp templates to large lists. If your primary motion is outbound marketing campaigns, that focus shows.
- **Shared team inbox.** Multiple agents can work the same WABA number from one screen, which beats a single phone passed around the office.
- **Catalog and commerce features.** For D2C and retail sellers who want product catalogs inside WhatsApp, DoubleTick leans into that use case.
- **Official WABA compliance.** Because it runs on the WhatsApp Cloud API, bulk sending goes through Meta's approved template system rather than risky personal-number blasting — the safe way to reach thousands.

If your team is essentially a broadcasting operation, DoubleTick is a reasonable tool. The friction shows up when WhatsApp is supposed to _feed a sales pipeline_ rather than just push campaigns.

## Where DoubleTick Falls Short For CRM-Led Teams

Here are the three complaints that come up most often from teams evaluating a **DoubleTick alternative** — kept factual, not exaggerated.

**1. Per-conversation billing adds up.** Like most API-BSPs, DoubleTick sits on top of Meta's WABA pricing and bills around conversations/messages. For high-volume teams, the line item grows fast — and it's about to matter more, because **Meta resumes charging for service messages on the API from October 1, 2026** (these were free from November 1, 2024, so it's a resumption, not a brand-new fee). Teams that only need to keep _manual_ conversations flowing are increasingly asking whether they need to route everything through the paid API at all.

**2. "It's a broadcast tool, not a CRM."** This is the exact sentiment behind the demo quote at the top. WABA tools can log activity, but they don't natively write two-way into Zoho, HubSpot, or Salesforce records. Reps end up copy-pasting, and managers lose the thread. One buyer summed up the goal: _"one solution for everything… everything in a single window."_

**3. Limited analytics on lower plans.** Response time, follow-up tracking, and rep-level performance are often reserved for higher tiers or simply absent. For a sales manager who wants to know _who replied, who dropped a lead_, that gap is the whole point of buying a tool.

None of this makes DoubleTick a bad product. It makes it a _broadcast_ product being asked to do _CRM_ work.

## Two-Way CRM Sync vs a Broadcast-First Tool

This is the fault line between the two tools, so it deserves its own section.

DoubleTick is excellent at pushing messages _out_. But CRM-led teams need the reverse too: the conversation has to flow _back into the record_ so the pipeline stays accurate without anyone re-typing it.

With Eazybe's **Zoho CRM integration**, synced WhatsApp conversations land in the **Notes** section of the matching Contact and Lead, and into the **5 latest deals** of that contact. The **Zoho Mini-CRM View** pulls Zoho _inside_ the WhatsApp interface — create leads, edit profiles, log notes, assign tasks, and manage deals without leaving the chat. A **"Go to Zoho"** button jumps to the record; **"Go Back to WhatsApp"** jumps back. That's the _"start chatting from the CRM itself… I don't have to switch back to WhatsApp"_ experience buyers describe.

Crucially, the standard Zoho backup and Mini-CRM run over the **Chrome extension on WhatsApp Web — no WABA required**. Sync happens automatically on short cadences (the docs reference ~15-minute contact syncing, while the installable Zoho backup Component runs chat backup about every 3 minutes — so it's near-real-time, not batched overnight; the two figures cover different things, so there isn't a single unified interval).

HubSpot users get the same shape: conversations appear under **WhatsApp Activity** on the contact timeline, the latest associated Deal, and the Company page, plus a HubSpot Mini-CRM View and a **"Send WhatsApp message"** workflow action. You can even build a HubSpot dashboard on Eazybe data sources like **Average Response Time**, **First Response Time**, **Follow-ups**, and **Last AI Summary**.

That's the difference in one line: a broadcast tool ends the conversation; a CRM-synced tool keeps it.

## Keep Your Number: Coexistence + No Migration

The single biggest fear we hear on switch calls isn't price — it's _"will my WhatsApp keep working?"_ One migrating team put it plainly: _"my WhatsApp should keep working after I disable coexistence."_

**WhatsApp Coexistence** is Meta's own solution for connecting an active WhatsApp Business App number to the Cloud API **without re-registration and without losing chats or contacts**. The docs call it "the safest method to migrate to Cloud API without losing data." The same number works across the free **Business App** and the **Cloud API** at the same time, with real-time sync in both directions — a message you send from your phone shows up in the API inbox, and vice versa.

What that unlocks for a DoubleTick switcher:

- **Keep manual WhatsApp** for informal, human conversations — the free Business App keeps working exactly as before, nothing is deleted.
- **Layer on automation** (broadcasts, AI agents, workflows) through the API on the _same_ number.
- **Avoid unnecessary API cost.** With Meta resuming charges for service messages from **Oct 1, 2026**, staying on the free Business App channel for manual chats is a real cost lever.

A few honest prerequisites, straight from the docs: you need **WhatsApp Business App 2.24.17+**, a verified **Meta Business Account**, a **Facebook Business Page** with admin access, and a number registered on the **Business App** (personal WhatsApp numbers aren't supported). The number must not already be connected to another WhatsApp API provider, and it should show genuine 1:1 activity for **3–7 days before and after** setup. Onboarding imports all contacts automatically, optionally up to **6 months of 1:1 chat history** and **2 weeks of media** — but **group chats are not imported**. And if you later unlink Coexistence, there's a **1–2 month cooldown** before you can reconnect that number.

## FAQs Related To DoubleTick Alternatives

**Is Eazybe a good DoubleTick alternative for Zoho users?**
Yes. Eazybe's Zoho CRM integration syncs WhatsApp conversations into the Contact/Lead **Notes** and the 5 latest deals, and its Zoho Mini-CRM View lets you work Zoho from inside WhatsApp. You can also recreate DoubleTick automations like payment-link sends using the `messageonWhatsApp` Zoho Workflow action (which installs an Eazybe private plugin).

**Do I have to migrate my WhatsApp number to switch from DoubleTick?**
No. Through **WhatsApp Coexistence**, Eazybe connects your existing WhatsApp Business App number to the Cloud API without re-registration and without losing chats or contacts. Your free Business App keeps working, and you can layer automation on the same number.

**Will manual WhatsApp still work after I switch?**
Yes. Coexistence runs the Business App and the Cloud API simultaneously on one number with real-time two-way sync. If you later unlink Coexistence, note the docs' **1–2 month cooldown** before reconnecting.

**How does Eazybe pricing compare to DoubleTick's per-conversation billing?**
Eazybe's core sync and Team Inbox are flat **per-seat** — Starter **$10/seat/mo** and Scaler **$15/seat/mo** (about 20% off annually), with a 4-day free trial. AI agents are a separate credit-based layer. Standard Meta messaging fees still apply to WABA broadcasts, and first-time WABA orgs get **₹100 / $1** in free wallet credit.

**Can Eazybe send broadcasts with delivery tracking like DoubleTick?**
Yes. With a connected WABA, funded wallet, and approved templates, Eazybe sends broadcasts and tracks **Sent, Delivered, Read, and Failed** status plus response tracking and cost analysis. Broadcasts require opted-in contacts and Meta-approved templates.

**Does Eazybe give managers visibility into rep performance?**
Yes. The **Team Inbox** uses Admin/Manager/Agent roles (agents see only their own chats), and analytics cover response time, follow-ups, last-active, and leaderboards. On the **Scaler** plan, an AI agent triages unreplied chats as Critical/Non-Critical and sends admins WhatsApp summaries roughly every three hours (customizable to business hours).

**Does Eazybe store my WhatsApp messages on its own servers?**
No. Eazybe is a connector — chat data syncs into your connected CRM or backs up to your own Google Drive (Team Inbox path). It doesn't store chats or media on Eazybe servers, and data in transit is encrypted over TLS/HTTPS.

**Is the CRM sync only available with the WhatsApp Business API?**
No. The standard Zoho/HubSpot backup and Mini-CRM run over the **Chrome extension on WhatsApp Web** — WABA is a separate optional layer you add only when you want compliant, large-volume broadcasts.
