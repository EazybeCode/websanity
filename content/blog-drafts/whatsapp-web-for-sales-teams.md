# How to Use WhatsApp Web for Sales Teams (2026 Guide)

Your sales team operates on WhatsApp. Every lead messages at midnight. Every deal thread stretches across 47 messages. And somewhere in the chaos, hot leads slip through because your reps are drowning in a flat, unfiltered chat list where "urgent buyer ready to sign" looks identical to "spam status downloader".

Meanwhile, your CRM sits idle — because copying every WhatsApp conversation into HubSpot or Zoho by hand takes 20 minutes per lead, and your team has 30 active chats going at once.

The problem isn't WhatsApp. It's that **WhatsApp Web was built for personal chats, not sales operations**. Out of the box, it has no filters, no CRM sync, no AI triage, and no way to tell which conversations are about to close vs which ones went cold three days ago.

But with the right tools, WhatsApp Web can become your **operating system for sales and marketing** — complete with AI properties that auto-classify chats, stackable filters that surface hot leads, CRM sync that logs every message, and a virtual assistant that prevents missed follow-ups.

This guide shows you how.

## What's Wrong With WhatsApp Web for Sales (Out of the Box)

WhatsApp Web is great for chatting with friends. It's terrible for managing 30 sales conversations at once.

**The problems:**

1. **Everything mixes together** — personal chats, customer inquiries, spam messages, hot leads all appear in one flat list
2. **No visibility into what's urgent** — "I'm ready to sign" and "Can you send the brochure?" look identical
3. **No team coordination** — when 5 reps handle the same inbox, nobody knows who's replying to whom
4. **No CRM sync** — every conversation must be manually copy-pasted into HubSpot/Zoho/Salesforce
5. **Chats slip through** — a lead messages at 2 AM; by 9 AM it's buried under 20 new chats
6. **No analytics** — you can't answer "What's our average response time?" or "Which rep closed the most deals?"

**Bottom line:** WhatsApp Web is a chat app, not a sales workspace. But it *can* become one.

1. Click **Integrations** → select your CRM (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Pipedrive, etc.)
2. Authorize Eazybe to access your CRM data
3. Contacts auto-match on phone number
4. Chats start syncing every ~3 min (initial backup: past 3 days)

### Step 3: Enable AI Properties

1. Click **AI Features** → **AI Sales Brief**
2. Choose which properties to enable: Summary, Intent, Urgency, Objection, Next Action
3. Map AI properties to CRM custom fields (e.g., Intent → HubSpot "WhatsApp Intent" property)

### Step 4: Set Up Team Inbox

1. Click **Team** → **Invite Members**
2. Assign roles: Admin, Manager, Agent
3. Turn on **Chat Assignment** (reps can claim chats or admins can assign)
4. Turn on **Unreplied filter** and **Virtual assistant** (digest every 3 hours)

### Step 5: Create Saved Views (Filters)

1. Click **Filters** → stack filters (e.g., Intent = Purchase + Urgency = High + Assigned to Me)
2. Click **Save as View** → name it "My Hot Leads"
3. One-click access to your priority queue

### Step 6: Deploy AI Agent (Optional)

1. Click **AI Agents** → **Create New Agent**
2. Describe what it should do: "Answer FAQ questions about shipping, returns, and product specs. Transfer to human if customer wants to negotiate bulk pricing."
3. Upload Knowledge Base (FAQs, product docs)
4. Choose **Light LLM** (fast, cheap) or **Heavy LLM** (better reasoning)

### Step 7: Run Your First Broadcast (Optional, WABA Required)
