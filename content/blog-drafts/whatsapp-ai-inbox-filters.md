## What Are AI-Powered WhatsApp Inbox Filters?

Traditional WhatsApp doesn't let you filter chats by business context. You see *everything*—the customer asking for pricing, the vendor sending invoices, the team member sharing memes—in one endless scroll.

**AI-powered inbox filters** solve this by:

1. **AI properties** — Automatically analyzing each chat to extract business signals (intent, urgency, objection type, next action needed).
2. **CRM-driven Dynamic Labels** — Syncing HubSpot, Zoho, or Salesforce properties (deal stage, contact lifecycle, custom fields) into WhatsApp as filterable labels.
3. **Stackable filters** — Combining multiple criteria (channel + assignee + AI intent + CRM deal stage) to create laser-focused views of your inbox.

Instead of manually reading 200 chats to find the 10 that matter, you filter by "High Urgency + Open Deal + Unassigned" and instantly see what needs a reply.

## Messaging Window Filter: Avoid API Charges

Meta's per-message pricing (enforced since November 2025) makes the **messaging window filter** critical for cost control.

If a chat is **outside the 24-hour window**, you can only message the customer using a pre-approved template—and you'll pay per message. The messaging window filter lets you prioritize chats where the customer recently replied (window still open), so you can send free replies instead of paid template messages.

Filter logic:

- **"Messaging Window = Open"** → Customer replied within 24 hours; you can send free-form replies.
- **"Messaging Window = Closed"** → Customer hasn't replied in 24+ hours; you'll need a template (and pay per message).

For teams using coexistence (one number on both the Business App and API), this filter is a money-saver: route "Window Closed" chats to the Business App channel (where all messages are free) instead of the API.

## Honest Limitations: What AI Filters Can't Do

AI-powered filters are assistive tools—they help humans prioritize, not replace human judgment. Here's what to know:

1. **AI properties are confidence-scored, not perfect.** The system might label a sarcastic "sure, I'll pay $10,000" as High Purchase Intent when it's actually an objection. Always review AI-tagged chats before acting.

2. **CRM sync is one-directional.** Dynamic Labels pull data from HubSpot/Zoho into WhatsApp, but tagging a chat in WhatsApp won't update the CRM. For two-way sync, you'll need to update the CRM directly (Eazybe writes some properties like "Last WhatsApp Message" back to the CRM, but not custom tags).

3. **Filter precision depends on your CRM hygiene.** If your HubSpot deals are miscategorized or outdated, the WhatsApp filters will inherit those errors. Clean CRM data = accurate filters.

4. **Initial AI property assignment takes 6 minutes.** When a new chat arrives, it won't have AI properties until the next scan cycle. For instant triage, combine AI filters with manual assignment rules.

5. **Dynamic Labels require field mapping.** You need to configure which CRM properties sync to WhatsApp (e.g., "Deal Stage" becomes a WhatsApp label). This is a one-time setup but requires admin access to both systems.
