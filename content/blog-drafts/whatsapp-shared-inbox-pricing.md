# WhatsApp Shared Inbox Pricing: Complete 2026 Cost Comparison

WhatsApp shared inbox tools help teams manage customer conversations, but pricing varies wildly across providers. Some charge per user, others per conversation, and many hide costs like Meta API charges, BSP markups, and AI add-ons. This guide breaks down WhatsApp shared inbox pricing models, compares major providers, and explains what you'll actually pay in 2026.

**TL;DR:** WhatsApp shared inbox pricing typically includes three components: (1) platform fee (per user, per conversation, or flat-rate), (2) Meta API charges (per message, from Nov 2025), and (3) optional add-ons (AI features, CRM sync, advanced automation). ## What Is a WhatsApp Shared Inbox?

A WhatsApp shared inbox is a platform that connects to your WhatsApp Business API number and lets multiple team members view, assign, and reply to customer conversations from one dashboard. Features typically include:

- **Team access:** Multiple users access the same WhatsApp number.
- **Chat assignment:** Assign conversations to specific reps.
- **Filters and views:** Filter by assignee, AI properties, CRM fields, or messaging window status.
- **CRM integration:** Sync contacts and conversations with HubSpot, Zoho, or other CRMs.
- **AI features:** Intent detection, urgency scoring, auto-replies.
- **Labels and funnels:** Organize conversations by stage or category.

Shared inboxes are built on top of WhatsApp Business API, so they incur both platform fees (from the inbox provider) and Meta API charges (from WhatsApp).

## WhatsApp Shared Inbox Pricing Models

Providers use three main pricing models: user-based, conversation-based, and flat-rate.

### 1. User-Based Pricing

**How it works:** You pay per user (or "seat") per month. Each team member who accesses the inbox counts as a user.

**Typical range:** $15-$50 per user per month.

**Best for:** Small teams (5-20 users) with predictable headcount.

**Example:**
- 10 users × $30/user/month = $300/month platform fee
- Plus Meta API charges (see below)

**Pros:**
- Predictable monthly cost.
- Scales linearly with team size.

**Cons:**
- Expensive for large teams (50+ users).
- Doesn't account for actual usage (inactive users still count).

### 2. Conversation-Based Pricing

**How it works:** You pay per conversation (or per message) handled. A conversation is typically defined as a unique customer chat within a 24-hour period.

**Typical range:** $0.01-$0.10 per conversation.

**Best for:** High-volume businesses (1,000+ conversations/month) with variable usage.

**Example:**
- 5,000 conversations/month × $0.05/conversation = $250/month platform fee
- Plus Meta API charges

**Pros:**
- Pay only for actual usage.
- Scales cost-efficiently for high volume.

**Cons:**
- Unpredictable monthly cost if conversation volume fluctuates.
- Hard to budget for growing teams.

### 3. Flat-Rate Pricing

**How it works:** You pay a fixed monthly fee for unlimited users and conversations (often with caps on features or message volume).

**Typical range:** $99-$500 per month.

**Best for:** Mid-sized teams (10-50 users) with moderate to high conversation volume.

**Example:**
- $299/month flat rate for up to 30 users and 10,000 conversations/month
- Plus Meta API charges

**Pros:**
- Simple, predictable pricing.
- No per-user or per-conversation overage fees (up to the cap).

**Cons:**
- May be more expensive than user-based pricing for very small teams.
- Caps on users or conversations can require tier upgrades.

## What's Included in WhatsApp Shared Inbox Pricing?

Most providers charge a platform fee plus Meta API charges. Here's what's typically included and what costs extra.

### Included in Platform Fee

- **Team inbox access:** Multiple users can view and reply to WhatsApp conversations.
- **Chat assignment:** Manual or automatic assignment to team members.
- **Basic filters:** Filter by assignee, date, or unread status.
- **Role-based access:** Admin, Manager, and Agent roles.
- **Basic analytics:** Message volume, response time, active conversations.

### Common Add-Ons (Extra Cost)

- **AI features:** Intent detection, urgency scoring, AI agents, auto-replies ($20-$100/month).
- **CRM integration:** Two-way sync with HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce ($30-$100/month, or included in higher tiers).
- **Advanced automation:** Workflow builder, broadcast scheduling, drip campaigns ($50-$150/month).
- **Dedicated onboarding:** Custom setup, training, and support ($500-$2,000 one-time).
- **White-label branding:** Remove provider branding from the inbox (enterprise only).

### Always Separate: Meta API Charges

**Meta (WhatsApp) charges per message sent via WhatsApp Business API.** These charges apply to all providers and are separate from the platform fee.

## Meta WhatsApp API Pricing (2026)

As of November 1, 2025, Meta introduced per-message charges for WhatsApp Business API. Previously, only conversations outside the 24-hour messaging window incurred charges. Now, all messages (inside and outside the 24-hour window) have costs.

### Message Pricing by Region (Per Message)

| Region | Marketing Message | Utility Message | Service Message (Inside 24h) |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|------------------------------|
| **North America** | $0.10 | $0.05 | $0.01 |
| **Europe** | $0.08 | $0.04 | $0.01 |
| **Latin America** | $0.06 | $0.03 | $0.005 |
| **India** | $0.02 | $0.01 | $0.005 |
| **Southeast Asia** | $0.05 | $0.025 | $0.005 |

**Message types:**
- **Marketing:** Promotional offers, product announcements.
- **Utility:** Order updates, appointment reminders, account notifications.
- **Service:** Customer-initiated conversations within the 24-hour window (e.g., support inquiries, FAQ replies).

**Key change:** Service messages (inside the 24-hour window) are now charged per message, but at a much lower rate than marketing or utility messages.

**Free tier:** Meta offers 1,000 free service conversations per month. After that, charges apply.

### BSP Markup

Some Business Solution Providers (BSPs) add a markup to Meta's API charges. Typical markup: 10-30%.

**Example:**
- Meta charges $0.05 for a utility message.
- BSP markup: 20%.
- Total cost to you: $0.06 per message.

**Always ask your provider:** "Do you charge Meta API fees at cost, or is there a markup?"

## Honest Limitations: What Pricing Doesn't Include

1. **Meta API charges are always separate.** No provider can eliminate Meta's per-message fees. They're built into WhatsApp Business API.
2. **Pricing caps can force tier upgrades.** Flat-rate plans often cap users or conversations. Exceeding the cap requires upgrading to a higher tier.
3. **Add-ons can double your cost.** CRM sync, AI features, and automation are often extra. Factor these in when comparing providers.
4. **BSP markups are hidden.** Not all providers disclose their Meta API markup. Ask before signing up.
5. **Free tiers are limited.** Some providers offer free plans, but they're typically capped at 1-2 users or 100 conversations/month—not viable for most businesses.

## FAQ: WhatsApp Shared Inbox Pricing

### 1. What's the cheapest WhatsApp shared inbox?

For very small teams (1-3 users, low volume), user-based pricing at $15-$20/user/month is cheapest. For high-volume teams, conversation-based pricing ($0.01-$0.05/conversation) can be more cost-effective.

### 2. Do I pay for both the shared inbox and Meta API charges?

Yes. The shared inbox provider charges a platform fee (per user, per conversation, or flat-rate), and Meta charges per message sent via WhatsApp Business API.

### 3. Is there a free WhatsApp shared inbox?

Some providers offer free plans (e.g., 1 user, 100 conversations/month), but they're too limited for most businesses. Expect to pay at least $50-$100/month for a viable shared inbox.

### 4. Can I negotiate pricing with providers?

Yes, especially for enterprise plans (50+ users or 10,000+ conversations/month). Providers often offer custom pricing, volume discounts, or bundled add-ons.

### 5. What's the difference between user-based and conversation-based pricing?

User-based pricing charges per team member (e.g., $30/user/month). Conversation-based pricing charges per customer chat (e.g., $0.05/conversation). User-based is predictable but expensive for large teams. Conversation-based scales better for high volume.

### 6. Are AI features worth the extra cost?

If your team handles 100+ conversations/day, AI features (intent detection, urgency scoring, auto-replies) can save hours of manual work. For small teams (< 50 conversations/day), manual handling is often sufficient.

### 7. How do I avoid hidden costs?

Ask providers:
- "Is CRM sync included or extra?"
- "What's your Meta API markup?"
- "Are there caps on users, conversations, or features?"
- "What happens if I exceed the cap?"

### 8. Can I switch providers later?

Yes, but switching can be disruptive. Export your conversation history, contact lists, and settings before migrating. Some providers offer migration support.
