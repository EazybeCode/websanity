## Why Your Pipedrive Is Always Out of Date

Pipedrive is only as honest as the last time a rep updated it. And reps update it last.

They update it after the demo, after the follow-up, after the "quick call" — if they update it at all. By then half the detail is gone, so the note is a shrug: "spoke, interested."

Multiply that across a team and a month, and your pipeline stops being a system of record and becomes a system of *optimism*. Deal stages don't match reality. Close dates are guesses. And leadership is forecasting revenue off data that nobody actually maintains.

The uncomfortable truth: **CRM hygiene isn't a discipline problem, it's a design problem.** You're asking the people paid to talk to prospects to also be data-entry clerks. They'll always pick the conversation.

## WhatsApp Is a Black Box (And Reps Won't Update the CRM)

WhatsApp makes it worse, because it's where the real selling happens now — and it's completely invisible to your CRM.

A prospect messages a rep's number. They negotiate. They ask for a proforma. They go quiet, then come back at 9pm. None of that is in Pipedrive. It's on one person's phone.

So you get two failures at once:

- **Leadership can't see the pipeline.** The signals that predict a close — intent, objections, next steps — are locked in chats no manager reads.
- **Reps won't transcribe it.** Even willing reps can't realistically retype 40 threads a day into CRM fields.

The chats are the most valuable sales data you own, and they're sitting in a black box. What you need isn't another nudge to "please update the CRM." You need a layer that **updates Pipedrive from WhatsApp** on its own.

## How to Update Pipedrive From WhatsApp in a Few Steps

Setup is a connection, not a migration:

1. **Add Eazybe** to Chrome and open WhatsApp Web — your chats stay exactly where they are.
2. **Connect Pipedrive** using your **Company Domain** (the subdomain in your Pipedrive URL) and your **API Token**, found under **Personal Preferences → API** in Pipedrive.
3. **Pick your fields** — choose which Pipedrive properties Eazybe should keep current, and which objects (Persons, Deals, Notes, Activities) to create from chats.
4. **Let it run.** From then on, conversations update the matching records in the background, and chats back up to the contact's Chat Backup view.

A Pipedrive account on **either a free or paid plan** works — you only need the Company Domain and an API Token to connect.

Not fully committed to a CRM yet? Eazybe can also mirror contacts and chat backups into **Google Sheets** as a lightweight home. It's honestly a simpler surface than Pipedrive — great for backup and labels, not a place to run a full deal pipeline — but it means small teams aren't left out.

## Honest Limits

We'd rather set expectations than oversell:

- **It's AI-assisted, not autonomous.** A human configures which fields sync and can review what's written. Treat the AI's read as a strong draft on the record, not an unquestionable verdict — especially on high-value deals.
- **Only the objects Pipedrive supports.** Persons, Deals, Notes, and Activities — **no Tickets, no separate Leads object, no call-log/event object,** because Pipedrive doesn't have them.
- **Backup is one-way.** Chat backup flows WhatsApp → Pipedrive so your CRM reflects the conversation; it's not a two-way messaging bridge inside Pipedrive.
- **Short chats carry less signal.** A two-word thread gives any AI little to summarize. The richer the conversation, the better the update.
- **Your data stays yours.** Eazybe is a connector and stores no chat data on its own servers — records live in your Pipedrive (or your own Google Drive). It's SOC 2 Type II, GDPR-compliant, and a Meta and HubSpot partner.
