# WhatsApp Number Migration: Move from Personal to Business API (2026)

## TL;DR

Migrating your WhatsApp number from personal or Business App to the Business API unlocks team collaboration, CRM integration, and cloud-based access — without changing your phone number. This guide covers embedded number migration (keep your existing number), coexistence (run Business App + API simultaneously), and how to preserve chat history and customer relationships during the transition.

The good news: you don't have to choose between your personal contacts and business features. Coexistence lets you keep both.

---

## What Is WhatsApp Number Migration?

WhatsApp number migration is the process of moving a phone number from one WhatsApp platform to another:

- **Personal WhatsApp → WhatsApp Business App** (small business upgrade)
- **WhatsApp Business App → WhatsApp Business API** (team access, CRM integration)
- **Personal WhatsApp → WhatsApp Business API** (direct enterprise upgrade)

The most common migration path: **Business App → Business API** for teams that need shared inbox, multi-agent access, and automation.

**Why migrate?**

- **Team collaboration:** Multiple agents access the same WhatsApp number without sharing a phone
- **Cloud-based access:** No dependency on a single device; team members log in from anywhere
- **CRM integration:** Sync WhatsApp chats to HubSpot, Salesforce, or Zoho automatically
- **Automation:** Build AI agents, broadcast templates, and automated workflows
- **Compliance:** Enterprise-grade security (SOC 2, GDPR) and audit trails

## Embedded Number Migration vs New Number Setup

### Embedded Number Migration

**What it is:** Moving your **existing** WhatsApp number to the Business API without changing the phone number.

**When to use:**

- You already have customer relationships on this number
- Customers recognize and trust this number
- You don't want to notify customers of a new contact number

**How it works:**

1. Your existing number is deactivated from personal WhatsApp or Business App
2. The number is registered with Meta as a Business API number
3. The API connection activates, and your team gains access via the platform (e.g., Eazybe)
4. Customers continue messaging the same number; they see no change

**Downtime:** Minimal (usually 5-15 minutes during the switch)

**Chat history:** Read the "Honest Limitations" section below — history preservation is not guaranteed.

### New Number Setup

**What it is:** Registering a **brand-new** phone number with the WhatsApp Business API.

**When to use:**

- You're starting fresh (new business, new sales team)
- You want to keep personal WhatsApp separate from business
- You need multiple WhatsApp numbers for different departments (sales, support, billing)

**How it works:**

1. Purchase a new phone number (local or toll-free)
2. Register it with Meta for Business API access
3. Configure your team inbox, CRM sync, and workflows
4. Start messaging customers from the new number

**Downtime:** None (existing personal WhatsApp remains active)

**Chat history:** No migration needed; you start from scratch.

## What Is WhatsApp Coexistence?

Coexistence allows you to run **WhatsApp Business App + WhatsApp Business API on the same number simultaneously**.

**Why this matters:**

When you migrate from Business App to Business API, you don't lose your personal relationships or Business App features. Coexistence keeps both active:

- **Business App** handles personal contacts, quick replies, and on-the-go messaging
- **Business API** powers team collaboration, CRM sync, and automation

**Use case example:** A sales manager uses the Business App for direct customer relationships and quick replies while on the road. The same number is connected to the Business API, where the sales team collaborates in a shared inbox, syncs chats to HubSpot, and runs automated follow-up campaigns.

### What Coexistence Unlocks

1. **Broadcasting:** Send bulk messages to customer segments (Business API feature not available in Business App alone)
2. **Template messages:** Pre-approved message templates for marketing, transactional, and service notifications
3. **AI agents:** Automate responses, qualify leads, and escalate to humans when needed
4. **Multi-agent access:** Entire team collaborates on the same WhatsApp number without passing a phone around

**Honest limitation:** While coexistence keeps both connections active, **only one platform can send a message at a time**. If the Business App sends a message to a customer, that conversation is locked to the Business App until the API sends the next message (or vice versa). This is a Meta limitation, not a platform restriction.

## WhatsApp Number Migration: Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Choose Your Migration Path

**Option A: Embedded Number Migration (keep your existing number)**

Best for: Teams with established customer relationships on WhatsApp

**Option B: New Number Setup (start fresh with a new number)**

Best for: New teams, or teams that want to separate personal WhatsApp from business

**Option C: Coexistence (run Business App + API on the same number)**

Best for: Sales teams that need personal contact access + team collaboration features

### Step 2: Verify Your Number with Meta

To migrate to the Business API, you need:

1. **Facebook Business Manager account** (free to create)
2. **WhatsApp Business Account** (register your number with Meta)
3. **Business verification** (Meta verifies your business identity via documents or website)

**Timeline:** Business verification takes 1-5 business days (faster if you're already verified on Facebook).

### Step 3: Deactivate Your Current WhatsApp Connection (Embedded Migration Only)

**If you're doing embedded number migration:**

1. Open WhatsApp (personal or Business App) on your phone
2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete My Account
3. Confirm deletion (this deactivates the number from personal/Business App)

**Important:** Once you delete your account, you have **30 days** to activate the number on the Business API before Meta releases it. Don't wait too long.

**If you're doing coexistence:** You do **not** delete your Business App account. The platform (e.g., Eazybe) handles the coexistence setup via Meta's API.

### Step 4: Connect Your Number to the Business API Platform

**Option 1: Scan QR code (WhatsApp Web extension)**

If you're using Eazybe's Chrome extension:

1. Open the Eazybe platform
2. Click "Connect WhatsApp"
3. Scan the QR code with your phone (same process as WhatsApp Web)
4. Your number is now connected to the shared inbox

**Option 2: Embedded signup (Business API registration)**

If you're migrating to the Business API via Meta's embedded signup:

1. Click "Connect via Business API"
2. Log in to your Facebook Business Manager
3. Select your WhatsApp Business Account
4. Authorize the platform (e.g., Eazybe) to access your WhatsApp number
5. Meta activates your number on the API (5-15 minutes)

### Step 5: Configure Team Access and Roles

Assign team members to roles:

- **Admin:** Full access, can configure automation, assign tickets, manage team
- **Manager:** Oversight access, can view all chats and reassign tickets
- **Agent:** Access only to assigned chats

Set working hours and availability rules to control when agents receive new chats.

### Step 6: Sync Your CRM (Optional but Recommended)

Connect HubSpot, Salesforce, or Zoho to sync:

- WhatsApp chats to contact records
- Contact properties (deal stage, lifecycle stage) to WhatsApp inbox filters
- Automated workflows (e.g., send WhatsApp follow-up when deal stage changes)

**Sync frequency:**

- HubSpot/Salesforce: ~3 minutes
- Zoho contacts: ~15 minutes

**Chat history sync:** The platform backs up the **last 3 days** of WhatsApp chats to your CRM by default. Older messages are not synced unless you configure a custom backup.

### Step 7: Test with a Small Group

Before migrating all customer conversations:

1. Send a test message to your own phone number (verify it appears in the team inbox)
2. Assign a test chat to an agent (verify assignment works)
3. Reply from the platform and confirm the customer receives it
4. Check CRM sync (verify the message logs to HubSpot/Salesforce/Zoho)

Once testing succeeds, notify your team and start handling live customer chats.

## Honest Limitations of WhatsApp Number Migration

### Chat History Is Not Guaranteed to Migrate

Meta does **not** provide an official API to export or migrate WhatsApp chat history from personal/Business App to Business API. Some platforms (including Eazybe) back up the **last 3 days** of chats during migration, but older messages may not transfer.

**What this means:**

- If you migrate on Day 1, you'll have access to chats from Days -2, -1, and 0
- Chats older than 3 days are not accessible via the API (they remain on your phone until you delete the app)

**Workaround:** Before migrating, manually export critical chat history via WhatsApp's "Export Chat" feature (Settings → Chats → Chat History → Export). Save the .txt or .zip file to Google Drive or your CRM.

### Only One Platform Can Send at a Time (Coexistence)

When using coexistence, WhatsApp locks each conversation to the platform that sent the **most recent message**. If your Business App sends a reply, that customer's chat is locked to the Business App until the API sends the next message.

**Why this matters:** Your team can't reply from the shared inbox (API) if the customer's last message came through the Business App. You'll need to coordinate which platform handles which conversations.

**Best practice:** Use the Business App for personal contacts and quick on-the-go replies. Use the Business API (team inbox) for all customer support and sales workflows.

### 24-Hour Messaging Window Applies After Migration

Once you migrate to the Business API, Meta's **24-hour messaging window** rule applies:

- **Within 24 hours of the customer's last message:** You can send freeform replies
- **After 24 hours:** You must use pre-approved **template messages**

Personal WhatsApp and Business App do **not** have this restriction. If you're used to messaging customers anytime, you'll need to adjust workflows or use template messages for follow-ups.

### Number Cannot Be Used on Multiple Devices (Non-Coexistence)

If you do **not** use coexistence, the number can only be active on **one** platform at a time:

- If you migrate to the Business API, you **cannot** use the same number on your phone's Business App
- If you want both, you **must** enable coexistence

### Migration Blocks New Contacts for 24 Hours (Security Measure)

Meta enforces a **24-hour cooldown** after migrating a number to the Business API. During this period, you cannot send messages to **new contacts** who haven't messaged you first.

**What this means:**

- Day 0 (migration day): You can reply to existing customers but cannot initiate new conversations
- Day 1+: Full API access, including broadcasting and outreach to new contacts

**Workaround:** Plan your migration timing carefully. Avoid migrating on high-volume sales days.

## FAQ: WhatsApp Number Migration

### 1. Can I migrate my personal WhatsApp number to Business API without losing my personal contacts?

Yes, via **coexistence**. When you enable coexistence, your number stays active on both WhatsApp Business App (for personal contacts) and Business API (for team collaboration). You don't have to choose between personal relationships and business features.

Note: This requires the **WhatsApp Business App**, not personal WhatsApp. If you're on personal WhatsApp, first migrate to the Business App, then enable coexistence with the API.

### 2. How long does WhatsApp number migration take?

**Embedded number migration:** 5-15 minutes (after Meta verifies your business)

**New number setup:** Instant (once the number is purchased and verified)

**Business verification:** 1-5 business days (required for Business API access)

**Total timeline:** Plan for 1-7 days from start to finish, depending on Meta's verification speed.

### 3. Will my customers know I migrated to the Business API?

No. If you do **embedded number migration** (keep the same phone number), customers see no change. They continue messaging the same number as before.

If you set up a **new number**, you'll need to notify customers of the new contact number.

### 4. What happens to my WhatsApp chat history after migration?

Meta does not officially support chat history migration. Most platforms (including Eazybe) back up the **last 3 days** of chats during migration. Older messages remain on your device but are not accessible via the API.

**Best practice:** Before migrating, manually export critical chats via WhatsApp's "Export Chat" feature and save them to Google Drive or your CRM.

### 5. Can I switch back to personal WhatsApp after migrating to Business API?

Yes, but you'll lose Business API features (team access, CRM sync, automation). To switch back:

1. Deactivate your number from the Business API platform
2. Wait 24-48 hours (Meta's cooldown period)
3. Re-register the number on personal WhatsApp via the app

Note: Switching back and forth frequently can trigger Meta's spam detection. Choose your platform carefully.

### 6. How does coexistence affect the 24-hour messaging window?

The 24-hour messaging window applies to the **Business API** connection only. If you message a customer via the Business App (coexistence), the restriction does not apply — you can message anytime.

However, if the customer's last message came through the API (team inbox), you **must** follow the 24-hour rule or use template messages.

**Best practice:** Use the Business App for personal, ongoing relationships (no window restriction). Use the Business API for support tickets, broadcasts, and automated workflows (24-hour window applies).

### 7. Can I migrate multiple WhatsApp numbers for different teams (sales, support, billing)?

Yes. Many companies register separate WhatsApp numbers for different departments:

- Sales team: +1-555-SALES
- Support team: +1-555-HELP
- Billing team: +1-555-BILL

Each number connects to the Business API independently. You can manage all numbers in a single platform (e.g., Eazybe) with separate inboxes and team access for each.

### 8. What happens if I lose access to my phone during migration?

If you're doing **embedded number migration** and lose your phone:

1. Contact your Business API platform (e.g., Eazybe) immediately
2. Provide proof of number ownership (business verification documents)
3. The platform can recover access via Meta's Business Manager

If you're using **coexistence** and lose your phone:

1. Your Business API connection remains active (team can still access the shared inbox)
2. Log in to WhatsApp Business App on a new device and verify your number via SMS

**Best practice:** Enable two-factor authentication on your WhatsApp account before migrating.
