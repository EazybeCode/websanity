You signed up for Interakt to send WhatsApp campaigns. Broadcasts, order confirmations, abandoned-cart nudges — all going out at scale from an official API number. That part works.

Then your sales team asks the questions a broadcast tool was never built to answer: *Which of these replies is a hot lead? Who on my team is sitting on an unanswered buyer? What did the customer actually say, and what should we do next?*

That's the gap most teams hit. Interakt is excellent at pushing messages **out**. It's not designed to make sense of what comes **back** — the day-to-day sales conversations your reps are already having on WhatsApp Web. If you're searching for an **Interakt alternative** because broadcasting isn't the same as selling, this guide is for you.

## What Is an Interakt Alternative?

**An Interakt alternative is any platform a team adopts instead of Interakt to run WhatsApp for sales and marketing.** Because Interakt (by Haptik/Jio) is an official Meta WhatsApp Business Solution Provider centered on outbound API messaging, the alternatives split into two camps:

- **Other API BSPs** — tools like Wati, DoubleTick, and AiSensy that do the same broadcast-and-commerce job with different pricing and UX. If that's your lane, see our [WATI alternative](/wati-alternative) and [DoubleTick alternative](/doubletick-alternative) breakdowns.
- **Sales-intelligence layers** — tools like **Eazybe** that don't replace your broadcasting so much as sit on top of the WhatsApp your reps already use, turning conversations into CRM-ready signal.

The right pick depends on the job. If your only job is blasting templated messages to a list, another BSP is your comparison set. If the job is helping a **sales team** actually work the replies, you're looking for something Interakt was never built to be.

## What Interakt Does Well (An Honest Look)

Let's keep this fair — Interakt earns its place for the right buyer:

- **Official BSP status.** Meta certifies Interakt to provision WhatsApp Business API numbers, so you get a compliant, official path to bulk sending.
- **Broadcasting and campaigns at scale.** Bulk template campaigns, Click-to-WhatsApp ads, and segmented sends are its core strength.
- **Commerce flows out of the box.** Shopify/WooCommerce catalog sync, order confirmations, and abandoned-cart recovery are well-trodden.
- **A growing suite.** It has expanded beyond broadcasting into a shared team inbox, a chatbot/auto-reply builder, and a separately sold Sales CRM hub.
- **Low-friction start for SMBs and D2C.** For an emerging-market e-commerce brand that just wants WhatsApp marketing live quickly, it's a reasonable default.

If official-API outbound marketing at volume is your center of gravity, Interakt is a legitimate tool. The question is whether that's the whole job.

## Where Interakt Falls Short for Sales Teams

Reported by users and inherent to the broadcast-first model, these are the recurring gaps:

- **It's outbound-first, not sales-intelligence.** Third-party reviews flag limited workflow branching and shallow analytics — often little beyond delivery counts. Understanding *which reply is a buying signal* isn't the design goal.
- **The Sales CRM is a bolt-on.** Lead and deal tracking sits in a **separate subscription**, so the "sales" layer costs extra and lives apart from your messaging.
- **Costs scale with success.** Meta's **per-conversation** charges stack on top of the platform fee, and one third-party (Zoko) comparison cites a markup on conversation/template rates on non-Enterprise plans. The more you send, the more you pay.
- **Migration and setup friction.** You must move your number onto the official WhatsApp Business API — a separate number and app from the WhatsApp Web your reps live in — plus get templates pre-approved. Some users cite 15-20+ day onboarding.
- **Support and reliability gripes.** Recurring reports of slow post-sale support, intermittently failing automations, and billing/refund friction. (Review sentiment is polarized and thin, so treat these as user reports, not verdicts.)

A note on fairness: **per-conversation billing and ban/quality-rating risk are properties of the official WhatsApp API itself** — every BSP (Wati, AiSensy, DoubleTick, Interakt) inherits them. They aren't Interakt-specific defects. What *is* specific is the broadcast-first center of gravity.

On personal/Business App numbers, keep broadcast volume and opt-in discipline sensible regardless of tool.
