## Why Your LeadSquared Is Always Out of Date

LeadSquared is only as current as the last thing someone typed into it. And the person who's supposed to type is a rep mid-conversation, juggling ten chats, with a quota — not a data clerk.

So the honest workflow looks like this: the rep has a great WhatsApp exchange, promises to "update the CRM later," and later never comes. Multiply that across a team and every dashboard leadership looks at is a lagging, partial picture. Forecasts are built on stages nobody moved. "Update LeadSquared from WhatsApp" becomes a manual chore that quietly gets skipped.

The CRM isn't wrong. It's just starved. It can only reflect what gets fed to it, and the feeding stopped the day your team started selling in chat.

## WhatsApp Is a Black Box (And Reps Won't Update the CRM)

Two problems compound here.

**First, WhatsApp is a black box.** Leadership can't see into it. Deals, questions, objections, and next steps are happening in threads no manager can scroll. You're flying blind on your own pipeline because the pipeline lives in an app built for private messaging, not reporting.

**Second, reps won't update the CRM** — and it's not laziness. Switching from a live chat to LeadSquared, finding the lead, opening the right opportunity, and typing a summary is real friction during a real conversation. Any system that depends on that habit is going to lose.

The fix isn't nagging reps harder. It's removing them from the update loop entirely — reading the conversation directly and writing the result into LeadSquared without anyone stopping to type.

You don't want *everything* from a chat written into LeadSquared. You want the handful of fields your business runs on.

**6. What do I need to connect it?**
