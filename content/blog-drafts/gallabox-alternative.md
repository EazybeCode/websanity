## Where Gallabox Falls Short for Sales Teams

These aren't bugs — they're consequences of being a marketing-first BSP. Sales teams have different buying criteria.

### 1. Requires WhatsApp Business API Setup (No Personal/Business App Support)

Gallabox is **API-only**. You cannot use it on:

- Your personal WhatsApp number
- Your WhatsApp Business App number (on your phone)
- An existing number customers already message

You must onboard a **new WABA number**, get Meta approval, and often ask customers to message a different number. For sales teams whose entire book of business lives in existing chat threads, that's a migration they'd rather avoid.

### 2. Built as a Marketing Tool, Not a Sales Intelligence Layer

Gallabox logs chats and sends broadcasts. But it doesn't **auto-populate sales intelligence** into your CRM.

**What sales teams actually want in 2026:**

- **Intent** (is this a product inquiry, objection, or pricing question?)
- **Urgency** (is this lead hot or cold?)
- **Next Action** (should we send a quote, book a demo, or follow up in 3 days?)
- **Summary** (what did we discuss in the last 10 messages?)

Eazybe writes these **AI properties** (Analytics + Engagement Intelligence + Industry) directly into HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Pipedrive contacts/leads/deals. Gallabox logs the raw chat but doesn't extract structured sales signals.

**Example:** A lead messages "I need 500 units by next Friday." Eazybe's AI reads Intent: Purchase, Urgency: High, and writes those properties to the HubSpot deal. Your sales manager filters HubSpot by Urgency = High and sees every hot conversation — without reading a single chat. Gallabox would log the message but not auto-populate Intent or Urgency.

### 3. No Mini-CRM View on WhatsApp Web

Gallabox is a **separate inbox**. Your reps log into Gallabox's web app, not WhatsApp Web.

Eazybe is a **Chrome extension on WhatsApp Web**. Reps work in the interface they already know (WhatsApp Web), with a **Mini-CRM sidebar** showing the contact's HubSpot/Zoho profile, deal stage, and AI properties — all without leaving the chat.

For sales teams, this matters: reps don't want to learn a new tool. They want WhatsApp Web to *become* their CRM workspace.

### 4. No Coexistence Mode (API-Only)

**Coexistence** is a Meta feature that lets you **keep your WhatsApp Business App on your phone** while layering **Cloud API automation** on the same number. No migration. No number change.

Gallabox doesn't support Coexistence. You must choose: Business App (no automation) or API (no personal app access).

**Why this matters in 2026:** Meta now charges for **service messages** (Oct 2026), making API-only setups more expensive. Coexistence lets sales reps reply from the **free Business App** instead of burning API credits on simple replies.

### 5. CRM Integration Depth

Gallabox integrates with CRMs (HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive) via Zapier or native connectors. But the integration is **one-way** or **shallow two-way** (log chats, create contacts).

Eazybe does **deep two-way sync**:
