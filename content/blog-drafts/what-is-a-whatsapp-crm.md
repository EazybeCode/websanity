## What Is a WhatsApp CRM?

**A WhatsApp CRM is a system that manages your customer relationships directly inside WhatsApp — capturing contacts, conversation properties, pipeline stages, and team activity in one place — so WhatsApp Web functions as your CRM rather than a chat app you later copy into one.**

The shift is subtle but total. A traditional CRM with a WhatsApp plug-in treats WhatsApp as a *source* to be logged. A WhatsApp CRM treats WhatsApp as the *system of record* itself: the contact card, the properties, the labels, the funnel, and the reports all live where the conversation happens.

With Eazybe, that surface is the WhatsApp Web sidebar plus a shared workspace (the **Team Inbox**, also called the Revenue Inbox). Every chat becomes a record you can edit, classify, assign, and report on — without a rep ever leaving the tab where they're already working.

## Why a WhatsApp CRM Beats Bolting WhatsApp Onto a Traditional CRM

The old way asks reps to work in two windows and manually mirror one into the other. Nobody keeps that up, so the CRM slowly fills with stale, half-entered records while the real context stays trapped in chat.

A WhatsApp CRM closes that gap by making the conversation itself the input. Here's the difference, side by side.

| What you need | Traditional CRM + WhatsApp Plug-In | WhatsApp CRM (WhatsApp Web + Eazybe) |
|---|---|---|
| Where reps work | Two tabs: CRM + WhatsApp | Inside WhatsApp Web + one shared Team Inbox |
| Data entry | Reps retype chats into fields | AI reads each chat and fills properties for review |
| Classifying chats | Manual tags entered by hand | BEA Radar classifies by meaning: Intent, Urgency, Objection, Next Action |
| Segments | Static lists you update yourself | Labels, Funnels + Dynamic Labels that auto-add/remove contacts |
| Views & reporting | Only inside the CRM | Saved views in the inbox; properties sync to your CRM for dashboards |
| Team visibility | Depends on reps logging chats | Shared views + roles (Admin/Manager/Agent) and "Sent By" tracking |
| Setup | Number migration or API onboarding | No migration — personal, Business App, or API number |

> **The one-liner:** A plug-in copies WhatsApp *into* a CRM. A WhatsApp CRM makes WhatsApp *the* CRM.

## How a WhatsApp CRM Classifies Every Conversation

The engine that makes WhatsApp Web behave like a CRM is **BEA Radar** — Eazybe's AI property layer. It sits inside the Team Inbox, pinned beside each chat, and reads conversations for *meaning* rather than matching keywords. Analysis starts the moment a message arrives; there's nothing to trigger.

For every chat, BEA Radar writes three groups of structured **AI properties**:

- **The AI Sales Brief** — a fixed set on every conversation: **Summary, Intent** (e.g. *wants a demo*, *ready to buy*, *just browsing*), **Urgency, Objection,** and **Next Action.** A rep opens the chat and knows where it stands without scrolling.
- **Auto-created business properties** — fields learned from *your own* conversations, so they differ by business: Budget and Property Type for real estate, Plan Interest and Seat Count for SaaS, Destination and Travel Dates for travel.
- **Admin-defined properties** — an Admin adds a field, writes a one-sentence description of what to capture, and the AI reads it. Property types are **Text/Number** or **Dropdown** (a fixed option list like Hot/Warm/Cold).

This is the same "properties, not transcripts" idea we cover in the [WhatsApp Sales Intelligence](/blog/whatsapp-sales-intelligence) pillar — a conversation you can sort a whole pipeline by. And it works across personal WhatsApp, the Business App, and API numbers, plus group chats.

## Build Labels and Funnels That Auto-Classify Inside WhatsApp Web

Properties are the raw signal. **Labels and Funnels** are how you turn them into a CRM structure your team clicks through every day — all built inside WhatsApp Web.

- **Labels** are categorization. A contact can carry several at once, and they support sub-labels.
- **Funnels** are pipeline stages. A contact sits in exactly one stage at a time; moving it forward removes it from the last stage.

You create either from the **Add Label** button, set visibility to **Personal** (only you) or **Shared** (your team or the whole org), and apply them via the Label icon in the sidebar. Now for the part that makes it feel automatic:

1. **Saved AI-filter views.** Filter the inbox by any AI property — say *Intent = ready to buy* with an empty *Objection* — then **Save as View.** New chats that match get classified into that view automatically as BEA Radar reads them. It's a live, self-updating segment.
2. **Dynamic Labels.** These auto-generate from rules you set on CRM properties (a module + property + condition + value). Because AI properties also sync into your connected CRM, a Dynamic Label can ride on an AI-derived field — a contact is added the moment it matches and removed when it doesn't, in real time.

So "auto-classifying labels based on the conversation" isn't hand-waving: BEA Radar reads the chat, the property updates, and your view or Dynamic Label re-sorts itself.

## Turn Those Properties Into Views, Charts, and Dashboards

A CRM isn't a CRM without reporting. A WhatsApp CRM gives you three layers of it.

**Views live in the inbox.** The all-filters editor supports operators like *is any of*, *is none of*, *is known*, and *is unknown*, with **AND** conditions in a group and **+ Add OR group** for alternatives. You see a live match count before you commit, then save the view — named, and visible to *Only me* or *Everyone*.

**Charts come from Eazybe's Dashboard.** It renders **Conversation Volume, Team Performance, Priority Breakdown,** and **Label Distribution** (conversations by label and funnel), plus metrics like Total Conversations, Unreplied Chats, and Average Response Time. Export to CSV.

**Deeper property charts come from your CRM.** BEA Radar itself doesn't draw charts from AI fields — it stays inside the inbox. But every property it writes syncs into HubSpot, Zoho, or Salesforce as a standard field, so it behaves like any other field in your CRM's dashboards and reports. On the deal side, **Deal Analysis** runs automatically every six hours, reading the WhatsApp threads and emails behind each deal and writing dedicated AI-prefixed fields you can filter, save, and chart — without ever touching your existing stage, amount, or close date.

## One Setup, Two Surfaces: Team Inbox and Every Rep's WhatsApp Web

Here's what ties it together. When an Admin builds a shared view, label, or funnel, it doesn't stay locked in a dashboard.

Shared saved views appear in the left sidebar of the **Team Inbox**. Labels and funnels appear in the top bar and inside each rep's **WhatsApp Web** sidebar. Build the structure once, and the same classification shows up for the manager reviewing the whole team *and* for the individual rep working their own chats.

Roles keep it sane: **Admins** see everything, **Managers** see their team's conversations, and **Agents** see their own. Assignment is manual and tracked — the "Assign to" list routes a chat to a teammate, and "Sent By" records who handled it.

## Honest Limits: The AI Is Assistive, Not Autonomous

We'd rather be straight about the edges than oversell them.

- **BEA Radar is AI-assisted.** Intent, Urgency, and Objection reads are strong prompts for a human — not verdicts. A rep still makes the call, and no classification is ever "100% correct."
- **A human configures it.** Admin-defined properties, Dynamic Label rules, and funnel stages are set up and reviewed by people. New properties only fill on relevant chats going forward.
- **Empty fields are normal.** If BEA Radar finds no signal, a property stays blank — that's expected, not an error. And manual edits to AI fields are overwritten on the next run.
- **CRM write-back needs a match.** AI properties sync when there's a connected CRM and an existing contact or deal to attach to. Managing AI properties requires the Admin role.
- **AI features are paid.** BEA Radar, AI Properties, AI Filters, and Deal Analysis sit on Eazybe's paid AI plans; CRM property-to-WhatsApp labeling is Scaler-tier.
- **Your data stays yours.** Eazybe stores no chat content on its own servers — conversations live in your connected CRM or your own storage. Eazybe is SOC 2 Type II, GDPR-compliant, and a Meta and HubSpot partner.
