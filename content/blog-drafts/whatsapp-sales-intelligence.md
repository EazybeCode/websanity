## What Is WhatsApp Sales Intelligence?

WhatsApp sales intelligence is a layer of AI-powered analytics and properties that sits over your WhatsApp inbox, turning every conversation into structured, actionable CRM data.

Instead of this:
> "Hi, I'm interested in your product. What's the price? I need it ASAP."

You see this (in your CRM or Team Inbox):
- **Intent**: Pricing inquiry
- **Urgency**: High
- **Timeline**: Immediate (mentioned "ASAP")
- **Next Action**: Send pricing proposal
- **Summary**: "Lead asked for pricing; urgent need; follow up with proposal today"

**The difference?** You don't have to read 247 messages to find the 3 hot leads. You filter your inbox by "Urgency = High" and "Intent = Pricing Inquiry"—the hot leads surface instantly.

### What Sales Intelligence Captures

**1. Analytics Properties (Measured):**
- Who sent the last message (you or the customer)
- When the last message was sent
- Average response time (per contact)
- Number of messages sent/received
- Days since last contact

**2. AI Properties (AI-Read):**
- **Intent**: "Pricing inquiry," "Product demo," "Support issue," "Complaint," "Competitor comparison"
- **Urgency**: Low, Medium, High (keywords: "urgent," "ASAP," "deadline," "need this now")
- **Sentiment**: Positive, Neutral, Negative, Frustrated
- **Objection**: "Price too high," "Needs competitor comparison," "Concerned about implementation time"
- **Next Action**: "Send proposal," "Book demo," "Follow up in 3 days," "Escalate to manager"
- **Summary**: One-sentence overview of the conversation
- **Custom properties**: Budget range, decision timeline, authority (decision maker vs influencer), product interest, region, industry—whatever you define

**3. Engagement Intelligence (EI) Properties:**
- **Heartbeat**: Is the deal still alive? (Active, Stale, Dead)
- **Escalation**: Does this need manager attention?
- **Task Creation**: Should you create a follow-up task in your CRM?

**Key Insight:** Everything is an AI property. If it matters to your sales process, you can define it, and AI will extract it from conversations automatically.

## Honest Limits: What AI Properties Can't Do

1. **Not 100% Accurate**: AI makes mistakes. It might classify "I'm thinking about it" as Low Urgency when the customer is actually high-intent. Always review and correct.

2. **Garbage In, Garbage Out**: If your conversations are vague ("Hi," "Ok," "Thanks"), AI can't extract meaningful properties. The better your sales reps qualify leads in chat, the better the properties.

3. **Requires Clear Instructions**: If you define a property as "Customer Satisfaction" but don't specify how to measure it, AI will guess. Be specific: "Classify as Satisfied if customer says 'great,' 'thanks,' 'happy'; Unsatisfied if 'disappointed,' 'frustrated,' 'refund.'"

4. **Can't Replace Human Judgment**: AI surfaces signals. Humans close deals. If AI says "Urgency = High" but the customer is a known tire-kicker, trust your judgment.

5. **Sync Delay (3 Minutes)**: Properties sync to your CRM every 3 minutes, not instantly. If you need real-time visibility, check the Team Inbox in Eazybe (properties appear within seconds there).

If these limits block you, configure AI properties conservatively at first, test on a subset of conversations, and refine over time.
