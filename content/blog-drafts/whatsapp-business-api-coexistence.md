## What Gets Imported in Coexistence Setup

When you enable Coexistence, Meta imports:

### ✅ What DOES Import:
- **1:1 chat history**: Up to **6 months** of past conversations (optional; you can choose 0 days to 6 months)
- **Media**: **2 weeks** of images, videos, PDFs, voice notes from 1:1 chats
- **Contact names and profile photos**
- **Labels** applied in the Business App (converted to API-compatible labels)

### ❌ What Does NOT Import:
- **Group chats**: Group conversations do NOT migrate to the API side (you still see them on your phone app, but they won't appear in the API/Team Inbox)
- **Media older than 2 weeks**: Attachments from 2+ weeks ago don't import
- **Broadcast lists**: You'll need to recreate these on the API side
- **WhatsApp Business catalog**: Products don't auto-import; re-upload manually

**Note:** The 6-month import is a **one-time backfill**. After setup, all new messages sync in real time, forever.

## Coexistence Eligibility: Can You Use It?

Not every number qualifies for Coexistence. Meta has strict requirements:

### ✅ You CAN Use Coexistence If:
- You have a **WhatsApp Business App** number (not personal WhatsApp)
- Your app version is **v2.24.17 or higher** (check in Settings → Help → App Info)
- Your number is linked to a **verified Meta Business account**
- The number has been **active for 3-7 days** (not brand new)
- You're NOT in **Nigeria or South Africa** (Meta restricted these regions for Coexistence as of mid-2026)

### ❌ You CANNOT Use Coexistence If:
- You're using **personal WhatsApp** (must be Business App)
- Your app is outdated (update to the latest version)
- Your Meta Business account is unverified or flagged
- You already migrated to full Cloud API (Coexistence is for Business App users adding API, not the reverse)
- Your number is in Nigeria or South Africa

## Common Coexistence Setup Errors (and How to Fix Them)

### Error 1: "Your App Version Is Not Supported"

**Cause:** WhatsApp Business App is older than v2.24.17.

**Fix:** Update the app in App Store / Google Play. Restart your phone and try again.

### Error 2: "Meta Business Account Not Verified"

**Cause:** Your Meta Business account is unverified or pending review.

**Fix:** Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) → Business Settings → Security Center → Verify your business. This can take 1-3 days.

### Error 3: "Number Not Eligible for Coexistence"

**Cause:** Number is too new (created <3 days ago), in a restricted region (Nigeria/South Africa), or already on full Cloud API.

**Fix:** Wait 7 days if the number is new. If you're in a restricted region, you'll need to use full Cloud API migration instead.

### Error 4: "Import Failed—Group Chats Missing"

**Cause:** Group chats don't import in Coexistence (by Meta design).

**Fix:** This is expected. Group chats remain on your phone app only. For group chat CRM sync, see [WhatsApp Group Chat CRM Sync](#).

### Error 5: "Coexistence Disabled After X Days"

**Cause:** You manually disabled Coexistence or switched the number to a different Meta Business account.

**Fix:** Re-enable Coexistence—but note the **1-2 month cooldown** (see below).

## The Coexistence Disable Cooldown (Critical to Know)

If you **disable Coexistence**, Meta enforces a **1-2 month cooldown** before you can re-enable it on the same number.

**Why this matters:**
- Don't test Coexistence on your production number "just to see how it works"
- Don't disable Coexistence unless you're sure you want to go back to app-only
- If you switch Business Manager accounts, the cooldown triggers

**Best practice:** Test on a spare number first. Once you enable Coexistence on your main number, leave it on.
