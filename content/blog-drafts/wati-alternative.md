Your team already runs a CRM. Your reps already have WhatsApp numbers customers know and trust. So when a sales manager sits down to fix "we can't see who replied and who dropped a lead," the last thing they want to hear is: *migrate your number to the API, wait for Meta approval, retrain everyone on a new inbox, and start paying per conversation.*

That is the moment most teams start looking for a **WATI alternative**.

## What WATI Is Good At (An Honest Look)

A fair comparison starts by giving the incumbent its due. WATI is a legitimate, widely used product, and for the right buyer it's a good fit.

- **It's an official Meta Business Solution Provider.** Your API number, templates, and green-tick verification are handled through a sanctioned channel.
- **It's built for broadcast and support at scale.** Campaign broadcasts, opt-in management, and a no-code chatbot builder are core strengths, not afterthoughts.
- **It has a genuine shared team inbox.** Multiple agents can work the same WABA number with assignment and canned responses.
- **It ships CRM and e-commerce integrations.** Connectors to popular CRMs and Shopify exist out of the box.

If your primary job-to-be-done is *"send Meta-compliant marketing campaigns and run a support desk on a dedicated WhatsApp API line,"* WATI does that job well. This page is not an argument that WATI is bad. It's an argument that a large, specific segment — CRM-led sales teams — is structurally better served by a different model.

## Where WATI Falls Short For CRM-Led Sales Teams

The gaps below aren't bugs. They're consequences of being an API-first BSP. That's exactly why a different category of tool exists.

**1. It requires WhatsApp Business API setup.** You can't start on the number your reps already use. You onboard a WABA line, get Meta approval, and often move customers to a new number. For teams whose entire book of business lives in existing chat threads, that's a migration they'd rather avoid.

**2. Billing stacks on top of Meta's rates.** WATI's advertised subscription — the Growth and Pro tiers are commonly cited around **$59 and $119/month on annual billing** (verify current figures on wati.io/pricing) — is not your all-in cost. Meta's **per-conversation and per-template charges pass through on top**, and reviewers on G2 repeatedly flag "expensive" and "pricing issues." Add-ons stack too: extra users, chatbot sessions, and some integrations are billed separately. Aggregated reviewer sentiment (not a WATI-published figure) is that the real bill runs materially above the sticker. Treat that as attributed sentiment, not fact — but budget for the gap.

**3. It's a WhatsApp inbox, not a two-way CRM engine.** WATI integrates with CRMs, but the day-to-day work happens in WATI's inbox. For sales teams, the buying criterion in 2026 is *depth* of two-way sync — auto-logged conversations, contact matching, activity objects, workflow triggers — not just a connector that pushes a copy over.

**4. Support responsiveness is a recurring complaint.** Trustpilot scores run notably below G2, and reviewers cite slow or unresolved support. Weigh both sources together.

None of this makes WATI non-compliant or unsafe — it's an official BSP, and per-conversation billing is Meta's model, not a WATI trick. The point is fit.

## Native Two-Way CRM Sync vs Integrations

Here's the difference between *an integration* and *two-way sync*.

With Eazybe, WhatsApp and CRM contacts **link automatically through the phone number** stored in the CRM's Phone or Mobile field. Once linked, conversations **sync automatically every 3 minutes** — both 1:1 and group chats tied to that contact. On first connect, Eazybe backs up the **past 3 days** of chat so records aren't empty on day one.

Where the chats land, concretely:

- **Salesforce** — logged to the **WhatsApp Chats by EazyBe** custom activity object (for both Leads and Contacts), plus the Notes & Attachments section on Contacts. Attachments — images, PDFs, voice notes, video — are stored as clickable download links on the activity object.
- **Bitrix24** — logged to the **General section** of the contact's page. Only chats with a *linked* Bitrix contact are synced, which keeps noise out of the CRM.

Reps never leave their flow. A **Mini-CRM view** (open via the Salesforce or Bitrix icon in the extension's right sidebar) lets them create or edit a lead/contact, log tasks and events, and view profiles without tab-switching. A **One-Click Switch** — a "Go to Salesforce" button beside the contact name, and a "Go Back to WhatsApp" button inside the CRM — bridges both directions.

That's the answer to the demo pains: *"can WhatsApp numbers auto-sync/match with CRM properties, or is it manual?"* (auto, by phone number) and *"sync WhatsApp contacts without duplication"* (manual **link-to-existing-contact** options exist in the create flow, so you can attach to a record instead of spawning a new one). For teams evaluating the broader landscape, our roundup of the [top 7 CRMs with WhatsApp integration](/blog/top-7-crm-with-whatsapp-integration) covers where each connector is strong.

An honest boundary: **sending WhatsApp *from* Salesforce** is done via Record-Triggered Flows calling Eazybe's webhook, and that path **requires WABA, an approved template, and Enterprise+ edition**. Core chat sync, backup, and the Mini-CRM work on all editions through the extension — no API required.

## AI That Handles Off-Script Replies

Templated chatbots are great until a lead says something the flow didn't anticipate. That's where broadcast-first tools get brittle.

Eazybe's approach is rep-assistive rather than rep-replacing. **AI Suggestions** help reps rephrase and compose replies right inside WhatsApp Web, so an off-script question gets a fast, on-brand answer instead of a canned deflection. On the team side, the **Unreplied Chats AI Agent** surfaces conversations awaiting a response and flags **Critical vs Non-Critical** using urgency keywords (urgent, cancel, refund, complaint) and CRM customer value — with **Hot Lead & Escalation Detection** so managers catch the leads about to slip.

The contrast with WATI isn't "WATI has no automation" — it has a capable no-code chatbot builder. It's that a chatbot flow and a generative reply-assist inside the rep's own inbox solve different problems. For high-velocity sales conversations that go off-script constantly, the assist model tends to convert better than the deflect model.
